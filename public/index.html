How it works

You approve the standard once. We hold every turn to it.

  1. 1

    Confirmed the night before

    Your cleaner gets a text. No reply by our cutoff and we line up your backup.

  2. 2

    Photos on arrival

    Before touching anything, the cleaner photographs how the guest left it.

  3. 3

    Cleaned to your standard

    A phone link with an example photo for every angle, plus two close-up spot checks that change daily.

  4. 4

    Checked and fixed

    A person compares every photo to your approved examples. Anything off goes back to the cleaner, and we coordinate the fix before check-in.

  5. 5

    One report

    Ready, or what we found and what we did. If something was damaged, the claim packet is done.

What we don't do

We're the quality desk between your cleaner and your next guest.

  • ×We don't clean or do repairs. Your cleaner and your vendors do.
  • ×We don't replace your property manager or answer guest messages.
  • ×We don't buy supplies. We flag what's running low.
  • ×We can't guarantee every issue is fixable before check-in. When it isn't, you hear from us right away with options.
  • ×We prepare damage evidence, but Airbnb and Vrbo decide claims.

FAQ

Questions hosts ask

Something else? Email turnoverdesk@gmail.com and we'll reply within a day.

Do my cleaners need to download an app?

No. They get a link for each job and open it on their phone. About 18 photos, each with an example to match.

You've never seen my property. How do you know what clean looks like?

On the first turn, your cleaner photographs the finished unit. You approve or correct those photos and they become your standard. Every turn after is checked against what you approved.

What if my cleaner doesn't confirm?

We contact your backup cleaner and send them the same checklist. You only hear from us if neither is available.

I already use Turno or Breezeway. Do I switch?

No. Keep scheduling and paying cleaners the way you do. We work on top of it.

Do you restock supplies?

Your cleaner counts supplies on every turn and we flag anything running low in your report, so you can reorder in time. We don't buy supplies for you.

Who sees my photos, and how long do you keep them?

Only you, your cleaner and our team. Reference photos stay as long as you're with us, photos from any issue for 1 year, and routine turn photos for 90 days.

What if a problem can't be fixed before check-in?

We go step by step: your cleaner fixes it, then your backup cleaner, then we message you with the issue, photos and our recommendation so you can decide. Anything urgent, like a leak or a safety issue, goes straight to you and your local emergency contact.

What happens after the free pilot?

The pilot covers one property for up to 4 turnovers. If you want to continue, we send your first monthly invoice. Pay it and we keep going. If not, there's nothing to cancel.
