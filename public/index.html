FAQ

Questions hosts ask

Do my cleaners need to download an app?+

No. They get a text with a link for each job and open it on their phone. About 18 photos, guided by examples.

You've never seen my property. How do you know what "clean" looks like?+

On the first turn, your cleaner photographs the finished unit. You approve or correct those photos, and they become your Turn Standard. Every turn after is checked against what you approved.

What if my cleaner doesn't confirm?+

We contact your backup cleaner and send them the same checklist. You hear from us only if neither is available.

I already use Turno or Breezeway. Do I switch?+

No. Keep scheduling and paying cleaners the way you do. We work on top of it.

Do you restock supplies?+

Your cleaner counts supplies on every turn and we flag anything running low in your report, so you can reorder in time. We don't buy supplies for you.

Who sees my photos, and how long do you keep them?+

Only you, your cleaner and our team. Reference photos stay as long as you're with us, photos from any issue for 1 year, and routine turn photos for 90 days.

What happens after the free pilot?+

If you want to continue, we send your first monthly invoice. Pay it and we keep going. If not, nothing to cancel.
